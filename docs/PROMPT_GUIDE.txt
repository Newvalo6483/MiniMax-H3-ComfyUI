MiniMax H3 - Prompting Guide
==============================
For best results with MiniMax H3 video generation, structure your prompts
using the official format. H3-Context-IR (or your own preprocessing system)
expects prompts with specific sections.

PROMPT STRUCTURE

A well-structured MiniMax H3 prompt contains three sections:

1. integrated_multimodal_description
   - Shot-by-shot description of the video
   - Camera movements, angles, transitions
   - Character descriptions (appearance, clothing, actions)
   - Environment details (lighting, atmosphere, objects)
   - Temporal markers (e.g., "At 00:04.500, the camera cuts to...")

2. overall_soundscape
   - Diegetic sounds: wind, footsteps, engine noise, ambient room tone
   - Physical sounds: clinking, rustling, impact, creaking
   - Audio dynamics: crescendo, fade, sudden silence

3. non_diegetic_music
   - Genre and instrumentation
   - Tempo and mood
   - Dynamic changes (swelling, peaking, cutting to silence)

FORMAT EXAMPLE:

  [Shot 1] Cinematic, medium wide shot, pushing in slowly.
  In the cavernous bridge of a starship, sleek metallic consoles
  glow with amber displays. A female captain stands in the center.
  She wears a dark navy military tunic with silver insignias.
  Her back is to the camera, silhouetted against starlight.
  Outside the window, an armada of dreadnoughts hovers in formation.
  The fleet's thrusters begin to glow with intense blue light.

  [Shot 2] At 00:04.500, the camera cuts to a close-up of the
  captain's face. The blue-white light from the fleet reflects in
  her eyes. A blinding white flash floods through the window as the
  fleet jumps to hyperspace.

  overall_soundscape: A low resonant hum of the ship's systems,
  escalating electronic whine as the fleet charges. A massive
  deafening boom during the flash, metallic creaking of the bridge.
  Then abrupt silence, only the faint hum of the isolated bridge.

  non_diegetic_music: Cinematic space-opera orchestral score,
  slow tempo, mournful French horn over sustained string dissonances,
  building to a massive peak before snapping into silence.

REFERENCE-TO-VIDEO PROMPT FORMAT

For Ref2VA, use a structured format:

  subject_definitions:
  <Subject 1> is [description from reference image].
  <Video 1> is the source video for the editing task.
  <Audio 1> is [description of audio reference].

  summary:
  [Task type] The target video [description of what should happen].

  retention_analysis:
  <Subject 1>: fully_preserved - [what to keep]
  <Video 1>: partially_copy - [what to reuse]

  detailed_description:
  [Full scene description with dialogue using <d> tags]
  <Subject 1> (S1) speaks, <d>[English] Follow the wind, live free.</d>

  overall_soundscape: [audio description]
  non_diegetic_music: [music description]

TIPS FOR BETTER RESULTS

1. BE SPECIFIC: "warm golden hour lighting" not "nice lighting"
2. USE TEMPORAL MARKERS: "At 3.2 seconds, the camera begins to pan"
3. DESCRIBE THE SOUNDSCAPE: H3 generates audio natively, so describe it
4. MENTION CAMERA MOVEMENT: "slowly pushing in", "static shot", "tracking"
5. INCLUDE CLOTHING DETAILS: "dark navy tunic with silver chest insignias"
6. SPECIFY EMOTIONS: "her jaw clenches", "a relaxed peaceful smile"
7. USE SHOT NUMBERS: [Shot 1], [Shot 2] for multi-shot sequences
8. DESCRIBE TRANSITIONS: "the camera cuts to", "dissolves into"

LANGUAGE SUPPORT
H3 stably supports 11 languages. Use the <d>[Language] text</d> format
for spoken dialogue:
  <d>[English] Follow the wind, live free.</d>
  <d>[Chinese] 随风而行，自由自在。</d>
  <d>[Japanese] 風に従い、自由に生きよう。</d>
